# Adoption and feedback

Real-world feedback helps the project improve while keeping clinical data out of the repository.

## Share a privacy-safe adoption report

Open a GitHub issue with the following information:

- organization type, such as university, clinic, nonprofit, or independent research;
- workflow, such as teaching, dataset inventory, or DICOM de-identification evaluation;
- operating system and Python version;
- approximate number and formats of files tested;
- what worked, what did not, and the most valuable next improvement.

Do **not** include patient names, dates, identifiers, screenshots of clinical systems, real DICOM
headers, proprietary data, or information that could identify a person or institution without
authorization. Synthetic reproductions are preferred.

## What counts as adoption

An evaluation, classroom exercise, reproducibility study, integration prototype, or production use
can all be reported. Reports do not imply clinical validation or endorsement. With permission,
aggregated non-sensitive results may be summarized in project documentation.
