# Governance

`xray-mouth` is currently maintained by Douglas Casty as the lead maintainer. The project is
developed in public, and technical decisions should be traceable through issues and pull requests.

## Responsibilities

The lead maintainer triages issues, reviews contributions, manages releases, and makes final scope
and security decisions. Contributors who participate consistently may be invited to become
reviewers or maintainers as the community grows.

## Decision process

Small, reversible changes are decided through pull-request review. Substantial changes—including
file-format behavior, privacy controls, public APIs, and clinical-safety language—should begin with
an issue and allow at least seven days for community feedback when practical. Security fixes may be
handled privately and released without that waiting period.

## Independence and conflicts

Decisions prioritize user safety, privacy, reproducibility, and long-term maintainability. Relevant
financial, institutional, or dataset conflicts should be disclosed in the associated issue or pull
request.
